# London cycle atlas

A folded cycling atlas: a large geographic map, a quiet station index and a precise printed key. Selecting a station clears unrelated links and reveals directional spokes. Keep a consistent coordinate system and preserve geographic context.

The map leads immediately. Controls describe the visitor’s action in plain language. Analytical detail lives in a separate readable study. No artificial live indicators, no fabricated traffic and no official TfL branding.

Palette: ink #172c3d; muted ink #566874; vermilion #c64735; canvas #f4f7f8; paper #ffffff; water #b8d4e4; sage #dbe6d6. Community colors: vermilion, teal #267b76, indigo #5e65a6, ochre #a77723, rose #ac5781, blue #347fa5.

Typography: self-hosted Barlow Condensed 600/700 for title and section headings, Public Sans 400/500/600/700 for controls and prose. Tabular numerals for counts. No decorative monospace.

Layout: masthead across top, 322px map index on left, large unobscured map on right. Thin rules separate controls and ranked station index. On phones the map leads with compact heading and filters; station detail follows map. Study is a separate readable surface within the same app. Maximum visual noise: 80 OD links at rest; route threshold doesn't change analytical metrics. All available stations remain plotted.

Signature: selecting a station removes unrelated lines and reveals its strongest directional spokes. Quiet edges and readable proportional nodes, continuous geographic context. Clear legend and explicit dataset dates.

Quality floor: focus-visible, reduced motion, no hover-only access, mobile scroll, real loading/error/empty states, no fabricated live status, explicit routes-as-OD caveat.
